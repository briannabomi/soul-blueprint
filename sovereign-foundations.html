Questions

FAQ

Is this therapy?

No. This is coaching, spiritual development, somatic discernment, and intuitive/energetic work. It is not a replacement for therapy, medical care, mental health treatment, or crisis support. If you are in acute crisis or need clinical care, this is not the front door.

Do I need to be "spiritual" for this?

You need to be open. You do not need to identify as woo-woo. Some clients are extremely Type A and still get massive value from this work. But if you need to debate whether intuition, energy, the body, or the unseen are real, this is not the container for you.

Why three months?

Because this is foundation work. You need enough time to learn the practices, feel the signal, run the Soul Sprint, begin building the roadmap, and integrate the shift into actual life. One breakthrough is cute. A repeatable internal operating system is better.

Is ceremony included?

No. Private ceremony is not included in Sovereign Foundations. That is intentional. Ceremony without integration is irresponsible. Sovereign Foundations creates the prerequisite structure and capacity for deeper ceremonial work later.

Can I go straight into Sovereign Arc Mastery?

Possibly. But for most people, Sovereign Foundations is the cleanest entry point. If your application shows that the year container is a better fit, we'll have that conversation.

What if I already know what I want?

Then we'll find out whether it's signal, strategy, or protection wearing a cute outfit. Knowing what you want is not the same as knowing which part of you wants it. That distinction matters. A lot.
